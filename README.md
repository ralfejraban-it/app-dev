# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

> *"Friends don't lie."* — Eleven

---

## **Overview**
**Stranger Things** is a science fiction horror drama series created by the **Duffer Brothers**. It premiered on **Netflix** in **2016** and quickly became a cultural phenomenon.

---

### Key Themes
1. Friendship and Loyalty  
2. Government Conspiracies  
3. Parallel Dimensions (*The Upside Down*)  
4. Coming-of-Age  
5. 80s Nostalgia  

---

### 👥 Main Characters
| Character        | Actor                | Role Description                          |
|------------------|----------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Eleven           | Millie Bobby Brown  | A girl with psychokinetic abilities       |
| Mike Wheeler     | Finn Wolfhard       | Loyal friend and leader of the group      |
| Jim Hopper       | David Harbour       | Chief of police in Hawkins                |
| Joyce Byers      | Winona Ryder        | Determined mother of Will Byers           |

---

### Why I Like It
- Strong character development  
- Suspenseful and emotional storytelling  
- Retro 80s aesthetic and soundtrack  
- Clever mix of sci-fi, horror, and drama
